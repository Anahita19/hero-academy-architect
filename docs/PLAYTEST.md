# Playtest Guide

## Objective

Earn accreditation by clearing the two-rival tactical test. The academy plan is intentionally replayable: placement changes the opening geometry, while role choice changes damage and survivability.

## Planning phase

- **Vanguard / Astra:** 5 HP and a steady 2-damage Pulse Strike.
- **Flux Mage / Nova:** 4 HP and a higher 3-damage Pulse Strike.
- **Aegis Beacon:** a defensive module that marks a safe lane and can be placed alongside cadets.
- Click an open cell to deploy the selected role. Occupied cells reject placement.
- Press **R** or click **Reset Layout** to restore the starting roster.

## Tactical phase

- Each turn begins with **2 action points**.
- Select a cadet from the roster or by clicking the unit on the grid.
- Click an adjacent orthogonal cell to move; movement spends 1 AP.
- Click a rival or press **Space** to Pulse Strike the nearest rival within 3 cells; the ability spends 1 AP.
- Pulse Strike has a visible 2-turn cooldown.
- Press **Tab** or click **End Turn** to let rivals move or attack.
- The teal overlay shows the selected cadet’s pulse range.

## Results

Clearing both rivals produces an accreditation result with score and percentage. From the result screen:

- **Revise Academy Plan** returns to the planning board.
- **Run Another Drill** restarts the tactical test with the current layout.
- **Return to Title** returns to the academy title screen.

## Controls

| Action | Keyboard | Pointer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start / confirm | `Enter` or `Space` | Click highlighted button |
| Select planning role | `1`, `2`, `3` | Click loadout card |
| Reset layout | `R` | Click Reset Layout |
| Move cadet | — | Click adjacent cell |
| Pulse Strike | `Space` | Click rival or Pulse Strike |
| End turn | `Tab` | Click End Turn |
| Back out | `Escape` | — |
